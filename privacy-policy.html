Privacy Policy for Apps, Websites, and Software Developments

This policy explains how information is handled across websites, mobile applications, web applications, and software solutions developed or maintained by Eben Varghese Paul.

Last updated: July 3, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to digital products, websites, mobile apps, web applications, prototypes, and related software services developed, published, or maintained by Eben Varghese Paul unless a specific product provides its own privacy policy.

If an individual app or client project includes a separate privacy notice, that app-specific or project-specific notice will take priority for that product.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the product or service, we may collect information that users provide directly, such as name, email address, phone number, messages, account details, support requests, or form submissions.

Some applications may also process operational information such as device type, browser type, IP address, app version, crash logs, usage events, authentication status, and technical diagnostics required to keep the service reliable and secure.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide requested services, operate software features, respond to support requests, improve reliability, troubleshoot issues, secure systems, maintain records, and communicate important updates.

We do not sell personal information. Data is used only for legitimate operational, support, security, development, or legal purposes connected to the relevant product or service.

4. App Permissions

Some apps may request device permissions such as camera, storage, location, notifications, contacts, or network access. Permissions are requested only when needed for a specific feature.

Users can manage or revoke app permissions through their device settings. Disabling certain permissions may limit related app functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

Products may use third-party platforms for hosting, analytics, authentication, payments, notifications, maps, crash reporting, cloud storage, email delivery, or infrastructure. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

Examples may include cloud providers, app stores, analytics tools, payment gateways, messaging services, or API providers, depending on the specific product.

6. Data Sharing

Information may be shared only when required to operate the product, provide support, comply with law, protect security, complete a business process requested by the user, or work with trusted service providers.

We do not trade, rent, or sell user information for advertising purposes.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected, including service operation, support, legal, accounting, security, or maintenance needs.

Users may request deletion of personal information where applicable, subject to legal, contractual, security, or operational requirements.

8. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no digital system can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

Users are encouraged to use strong passwords, keep devices updated, and avoid sharing sensitive credentials.

9. Children's Privacy

Products are not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children unless the specific app or service clearly states otherwise and includes appropriate safeguards.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information, they may contact us to request review or deletion.

10. User Rights

Depending on the applicable law and product context, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of their personal information.

Requests can be sent using the contact details below. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

11. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in products, services, technologies, or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
